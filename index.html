Common Questions About Our Dental Services

What should I do in case of a dental emergency?
If you have a dental emergency, contact our clinic immediately. We'll guide you through the steps and schedule an appointment.
What are the signs I need to see a dentist?
Persistent tooth pain, bleeding gums, sensitivity to hot or cold, and visible discoloration are all signs you should book an appointment with us.
Do you offer teeth whitening services?
Yes, we offer professional in-office and take-home whitening treatments tailored to safely brighten your smile.
How often should I visit the dentist?
We recommend a routine checkup and cleaning every six months, though your dentist may suggest a different schedule based on your needs.
Are dental X-rays safe?
Modern dental X-rays use very low levels of radiation and are considered safe. We take every precaution to protect our patients.
